## What is Google Maps Screenshot Downloader?

Google Maps Screenshot Downloader is a lightweight Windows desktop app that lets you save a clean, high-resolution image of any location on Google Maps — without the search box, zoom buttons, Google logo, or place markers that a normal screenshot would include.

Instead of capturing a picture of the interactive map on screen, the tool downloads the original map tiles directly from the source and stitches them into a single seamless image. Just draw a box around the area you want, pick a zoom level and map style, and download a ready-to-use image.

## How It Works

1. Open the app and paste a Google Maps link into the URL box, or move and zoom the map to the location you want.
2. Click **Edit selection** and drag a box around the area to capture, or skip straight to the one-tap **Screenshot** button for an instant capture.
3. Choose a **map tile source** (Road, Satellite, Hybrid, Terrain, or an alternate provider) and set your export **Zoom** level, or leave Auto Zoom on.
4. Click **Download Map** to stitch the tiles into a single image and save it to your computer.
